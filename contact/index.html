Address, phone and hours

Bell Express Plumbing serves Bell, CA and nearby towns by phone. The phone number is (855) 575-2890, and the line is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For written questions about privacy or paperwork, use contact@expressplumberbell.com.

Plumbing requests are handled by phone only. If you are not sure whether a problem is urgent, call anyway and the dispatcher will help you judge.

What to have ready before you call

A few details make the call shorter and the visit more useful.

  • The full street address and ZIP code.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Where the main water shutoff is, and whether you have closed it.
  • The age and fuel type of the water heater, if it is involved.
  • Whether you own the property or rent, and if the landlord has been told.
  • A callback number in case the call drops.

What to expect on the call

The dispatcher asks short questions about the problem and the building. If water is spreading or gas is possible, you get safety steps first. Then the call moves to scheduling, and same-day appointments are offered when available.

No dollar price is given over the phone, since the plumber has to see the problem. You hear a clear price before work starts, and you decide then. Calls to the line may be recorded, as the terms of use explain.

Safety comes before the call

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and the gas utility from outside. For flooding, close the main valve, turn off power to wet areas if it is safe, and keep children away from sewage.
