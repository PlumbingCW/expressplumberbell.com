Water or Sewage on the Floor of Your Business

Act on safety first. Standing water near electrical equipment is dangerous, and sewage on a floor is a health issue for staff and customers. Close the area, switch off power to wet equipment if you can reach the panel dry, and post a sign.

Then find the water shutoff for the unit or building. In multi-tenant buildings the valve may be controlled by the property manager, so call them while you call us. Describe what you see to the dispatcher so the right plumber is sent.

Before the Plumber Gets There

These steps limit damage and help the repair move along.

  • Close the shutoff for the affected fixture or unit
  • Stop using sinks, toilets and floor drains if sewage is backing up
  • Move stock and paper records above floor level
  • Cut power to wet equipment only if it is safe to reach
  • Take photos of the damage and the source
  • Tell the property manager or landlord right away
  • Keep a written log of times and actions

Services for Shops, Restaurants and Property Managers

Most commercial calls fall into one of these groups.

Grease trap cleaning

Restaurants and food prep sites need traps emptied and cleaned on a schedule. The plumber checks baffles and outlets while it is open.

Storm drain cleaning

Roofs, lots and yards feed storm drains that fill with leaves and silt. Clearing them keeps water from pooling at doors and docks.

Backflow testing

Devices that guard the water supply are tested by a licensed plumber. Ask your water company how often yours is due.

Commercial drain service

Floor drains, mop sinks, restroom lines and main drains are cleaned by cabling or jetting.

Hunting Down a Leak Inside a Business

Commercial leaks hide above ceilings, behind walls and under slabs. The first clue is often a rising water bill or a stained tile in the back room. The EPA WaterSense meter test applies here too. Read the meter, stop all water use for two hours, and read it again.

If the number moves, the plumber uses electronic listening equipment and pressure checks to narrow the search. Our leak detection page explains the method. A business that wants to know before damage grows can call ahead of a planned closing time.
