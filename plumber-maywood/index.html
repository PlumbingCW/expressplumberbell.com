Slauson Ave, Atlantic Blvd and the streets in between

Maywood's grid is built around two arterials. Slauson Ave runs east and west, and Atlantic Blvd runs north and south. City Hall stands at 4319 E Slauson Ave, and Maywood Riverfront Park at 5000 Slauson Ave sits beside the Los Angeles River with access to the river bike path.

Maywood Park, at 4801 E 58th St, has the Activities Center with a pool. The former Chrysler plant site at Eastern Ave and Slauson, and the old Willys-Overland plant at 6201 Randolph St, are reminders that the city grew up around industry. When you call, give the cross street so dispatch can place you on this grid quickly.

We also serve people who live on the edges of the city, near Huntington Park, Vernon and Commerce.

Small lots, extra units and shared lines

State housing data lists 6,768 housing units in Maywood. About 54.5 percent are detached single family homes, and typical lots run 6,000 to 7,500 square feet. Many lots also carry two or three extra accessory units behind the main house.

A drain or a service line that was sized for one household now often serves several. That is why a sewer backup in Maywood can involve more than one residence on a lot. Our sewer line cleaning visit starts by finding where the blockage is and who it affects.

The same data says that 65.8 percent of units were built before 1959, and 45.8 percent in the 1940s and 1950s. Ask the plumber to run a camera if you have repeated backups, because age and extra load make a repeat blockage more likely.

If you have a burst pipe or active leak in Maywood

Do these things while the plumber is on the way.

1

Close the main

Turn the main shutoff for your home clockwise. On a lot with extra units, ask everyone sharing the line to stop using water.

2

Keep away from sewage

If a backup brought wastewater onto the floor, keep children and pets out and avoid touching it.

3

Open a low faucet

After the main is closed, open one faucet on the lowest floor to relieve pressure and drain the lines.

4

Gather the details

Note which fixtures are affected and when it began. The plumber uses this to find the blockage or break.

Three mutual water companies, one iron and manganese fix

Maywood gets its water from three private mutual companies, Maywood Mutual Water Company No. 1, No. 2 and No. 3. In November 2021 the State Water Board marked the completion of a filtration system at the Maywood Avenue well that serves the No. 2 area, covering about 7,000 customers. It removes iron and manganese, which had exceeded secondary standards and caused odor and discoloration.

If your water looks stained or smells odd, check your bill to see which company serves you and ask them for the latest quality report. No verified hardness figure exists for Maywood, so ask your water company about scale before you buy a heater or softener. We do not diagnose health effects of water.
