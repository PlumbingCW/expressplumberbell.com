Water Spraying From a Pipe Right Now

Start with the main shutoff. In many homes it sits near the street side of the house, at the water meter box, or on the wall where the line enters. In an apartment, ask the manager for the building valve if your unit has no local one.

Turn the valve clockwise until it stops. Then open the lowest faucet in the house to drain what is left in the line, which lowers the pressure on the break. If water is near outlets, an appliance or the panel, do not step into it. Call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher what you see.

What to Do Before the Plumber Gets There

These steps limit damage and keep everyone safe. None of them require tools.

  • Close the main water valve and open the lowest tap
  • Turn off the breaker for rooms with standing water
  • Move rugs, boxes and electronics off the wet floor
  • Put a bucket or towels under active drips
  • Take dated photos and short video of the damage
  • Keep children and pets out of the wet area
  • Do not run the water heater with the water off

Pinhole Leak or True Burst

The two failures look different and the repair is not the same.

Pinhole leak

A tiny spot where the pipe wall wore through from the inside. It often drips or sprays a thin stream, and more pinholes tend to follow on the same run.

Split seam or crack

A lengthwise opening, often from pressure spikes or a pipe that was bent, knocked or strained at a joint. Water volume is much higher.

Failed joint or fitting

The pipe is fine but the connection let go. This is usually the quickest fix, though the neighboring joints need a look.

Damage from outside

A nail, a drill, a shifted wall or digging in the yard. The break is local, so a short section can often be replaced.

Repair Options by Pipe Material

The right fix depends on what the pipe is made of and how much of it is worn.

The order matters because a rushed patch over bad pipe tends to fail again.

What Usually Causes Pipes to Burst Here

Bell has a mild climate, so freezing is not the usual culprit. Bursts and leaks here more often trace to corrosion from the inside, high water pressure, age, a joint that was never seated well, or a nick from other work.

Hard water adds to the wear. For addresses served by Golden State Water, the 2026 water quality report lists an average hardness of 210 mg/L, which the USGS scale calls very hard. Scale builds inside lines and fixtures, and the practical tie-back is that older supply lines may deserve a look when a repair is already open. Ask your water company if you are not sure who serves you.

When a Repair Turns Into a Bigger Question

One burst on a pipe in good shape is a repair. Repeated leaks on old galvanized or tired copper may be a sign to price a repiping project, which the plumber can explain without pressure.

If the water is coming from under a concrete floor, the work changes. That calls for finding the leak first, and our page on slab leak repair covers those options. For leaks you cannot see at all, leak detection locates the spot before anyone cuts.
