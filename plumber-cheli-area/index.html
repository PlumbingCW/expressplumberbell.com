What the Cheli area is

The City of Bell describes the Cheli site as property east of the 710 Freeway. The city acquired land there beginning in 1997 and says it ultimately acquired 25 acres for industrial development. The surrounding parcels went to social service agencies and a school district.

Cheli is mostly a place of warehouses and industrial buildings, plus some institutional uses. The city's own planning page also notes an unplanned adjacency of industrial and residential uses. This page covers the industrial and commercial sites, not homes.

What is documented and what is not

Documented by the City of Bell: the location east of the 710, the 25 acres acquired for industrial development, and an active Cheli Specific Plan process. A Notice of Preparation comment period ran from July 2 to July 31, 2025, and the city page does not state an adoption date.

Not documented: the exact boundary in words, current tenants, building ages or which water provider serves each site. Published planning documents give different acreage figures for the plan area, so we do not quote one. Confirm details for your own building.

Services that fit warehouse and shop buildings

Drain problems come first in large buildings with many fixtures. Drain cleaning clears blockages in branch lines, and sewer line repair applies when a main line is damaged.

Hidden water loss under a slab or in a wall is a separate job. Leak detection helps locate the problem before floors are opened. For hot water in break rooms and restrooms, see water heater repair and water heater replacement.

Permits and who to ask

Plumbing work in California follows the California Plumbing Code. The City of Bell Building and Safety division at 6330 Pine Avenue handles plumbing permits. A tenant should also check with the property manager before any work, because lease terms often decide who pays and who approves repairs.

Large commercial sites may also have backflow or grease control requirements. We cannot confirm which apply to a specific building, so ask the city and your property manager.
